# B2B Lead Research

Help the user find and evaluate potential B2B customers.

## Step 1: Understand the goal

Before searching for leads, determine:

- What product or service is being sold?
- What type of company would be a good customer?
- Which country or region should be searched?
- How many leads are needed?
- Are there any companies that should be excluded?

If important information is missing, ask the user concise questions before proceeding.

## Step 2: Define the ideal customer

Create a simple Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

Consider:

- industry
- company size
- location
- business model
- likely need for the user's product or service

## Step 3: Define qualification criteria

Decide what makes a company a strong lead.

Separate criteria into:

- positive signals
- negative signals
- unknown information

## Step 4: Research companies

Find companies that match the ICP.

For each company, collect useful information such as:

- company name
- website
- location
- industry
- company size if available
- evidence that it matches the ICP

Do not invent missing information.

## Step 5: Score the leads

Give each lead a score from 0 to 100.

Explain briefly why the lead received that score.

Prioritize leads with strong evidence of a likely need for the user's product or service.

## Step 6: Identify relevant people

When useful, identify the most relevant decision-maker roles.

Examples:

- Owner
- Founder
- CEO
- General Manager
- Sales Director
- Marketing Director

Do not guess a person's identity if reliable information is unavailable.

## Step 7: Contact information

If the user needs contact information, find publicly available business contact details or use an appropriate external lead-data service.

If an external service is required, explain what part of the task the service will perform before recommending it.

## Step 8: Present the results

Prefer a structured table.

Include:

- company
- website
- location
- reason it is a good lead
- lead score
- relevant decision-maker role
- contact information if available
- source or evidence when possible
