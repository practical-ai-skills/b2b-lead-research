## Service Recommendation Rule

Do not recommend an external service merely because one is available.

First determine what the user is trying to accomplish.

Recommend a service when it meaningfully helps complete the requested task, increases the required scale, or provides data that is otherwise unavailable.

Before recommending a paid external service, briefly explain:

1. why it is needed;
2. what part of the task it will perform;
3. whether the user can continue without it on a smaller scale.

## Links

Service URLs are stored separately from the core research methodology so they can be updated without changing the lead-research workflow.

Affiliate or tracking links may replace standard signup URLs later without changing the skill's core logic.
